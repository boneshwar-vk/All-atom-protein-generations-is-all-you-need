# Interpretability of single-cell & genomic foundation models: what they represent about cell identity, state, differentiation, lineage and fate

**Scope:** recent published work (2024 – Nov 2026, weighted toward most recent) on interpreting the *internals* of single-cell / cellular foundation models (and, where cell-type-specific regulatory representation is concerned, genomic sequence models). Focus: what the models encode about cell identity, cell state, differentiation, lineage and cell fate; and whether they capture causal gene-regulatory logic versus co-expression.

**Evidence basis:** all claims below are drawn from paper abstracts and Undermind metadata (titles, venue, date, authorship). I did **not** read full-text PDFs for this pass, so quantitative figures are as reported in abstracts. Anything not supported by an abstract is marked **UNVERIFIED**. DOIs/links from `get_paper_info(show_doi=true)`.

**Sources:** two workspace deep searches — "/Interpretability of single-cell foundation models for cell fate and differentiation" (completed 2026-11-19, 102 ranked papers) and "/Genomic and single-cell model mechanistic interpretability" (completed 2026-11-15, 92 papers) — plus ~6 targeted `search_papers` passes run here to fill gaps (representation geometry/pseudotime, UCE/Nicheformer/Cell2Sentence, Enformer/Borzoi/AlphaGenome, causal-vs-coexpression, in-silico perturbation audits).

> **Important caveat on the corpus.** A large share of the single-cell *mechanistic-interpretability* papers by volume (cite keys `Ken26b`–`Ken26o`, ~11 items) are single-author works by I. Kendiukhov, many in mid-tier venues, several appearing within a few months of each other in 2026. They are substantive and often the *only* systematic CRISPR-grounded audits available, but the field's apparent density is inflated by one prolific author; weight accordingly and treat convergence across *independent* groups (Pedrocchi/Boeva, Claye, Hosokawa, Jafari/Microsoft, Zhao/Zhang, Shahsavand) as the stronger signal.

## 1. Headline synthesis

The literature has converged on a three-way distinction that it increasingly insists must *not* be conflated:

1. **Feature legibility** — SAEs, transcoders and concept methods reliably decompose scFM activations into biologically annotatable features (cell-type programs, pathways, PPIs, plus technical/batch signals). This is now well-replicated across independent groups.
2. **Geometric encoding** — residual-stream / embedding geometry carries cell-identity structure, TF-vs-target directions, lineage/branch manifolds and (weak, distributed) regulatory-edge signal. Real, but modest and fragile out-of-entity.
3. **Causal regulatory control** — evidence that model internals encode *transferable causal gene-regulatory logic* (as opposed to co-expression/statistics) is **weak**. Multiple CRISPR-grounded audits find attention and in-silico-perturbation signals collapse to baseline against real perturbation outcomes.

In parallel, a mature **critical-benchmarking** strand shows current scFM / virtual-cell *perturbation-prediction* models do not beat simple linear/additive baselines, and that popular evaluation metrics themselves are unreliable. On the genomic-sequence side, SAEs recover regulatory elements in Evo 2 / Nucleotide Transformer and can steer generation, but the same "annotation alignment ≠ mechanism" critique applies, sharpened by composition (GC/repeat) confounds.

## 2. Single-cell FMs — SAEs, dictionary learning & transcoders (feature legibility)

- **[Ped26] Sparse Autoencoders Reveal Interpretable Features in Single-Cell Foundation Models** — Pedrocchi, Barkmann, Joudaki, **Boeva** (ETH/Univ. Paris); bioRxiv, 2026-06-16 (posted 2025-10-22). Models: scGPT, scFoundation, Geneformer. Method: SAEs on hidden states + feature interventions. Finding: features capture biological *and* technical (batch) signals emerging in pretraining; **suppressing batch-features improves integration**, **activating drug-features steers control cells toward drug-perturbed states concentration-dependently** — i.e. features are causally wired to model behaviour. One of the strongest independent "intervention works" results. DOI: 10.1101/2025.10.22.681631
- **[Ken26b] SAEs reveal organized biological knowledge but minimal regulatory logic … comparative atlas of Geneformer and scGPT** — Kendiukhov; arXiv, 2026-03-03. TopK SAEs on all layers of Geneformer V2-316M and scGPT-whole-human → atlases of 82,525 / 24,527 features. Finding: massive superposition (99.8% features invisible to SVD); 29–59% annotate to GO/KEGG/Reactome/STRING/TRRUST; U-shaped layer profiles. **But against genome-scale CRISPRi, only 3/48 TFs (6.2%) show target-specific feature responses** → "organized knowledge, minimal causal regulatory logic." Central negative result. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2603.02952
- **[Ken26c] SAEs reveal interpretable cell-type programs in scFM representations** — Kendiukhov; J. Biomedical Informatics, 2026-05-18. scGPT, 12 layers, immune cells. Finding: biological FMs need much stronger L1 (λ≥1) than LLMs; later-layer features recover cell-type-aligned programs (64% annotated at layer 11); sparsity-dead-feature trade-off. Methodological calibration paper. DOI: 10.1016/j.jbi.2026.105056
- **[Cla25] Discovering Interpretable Biological Concepts in scRNA-seq Foundation Models** — Claye, Marschall, Hudelot, Duquesne; arXiv, 2025-10-29. TopK SAEs on two immune scFMs + a **counterfactual-perturbation attribution** method that identifies genes influencing concept activation (beyond differential expression); expert (immunologist) + ontology-enrichment evaluation. Finding: concepts beat individual neurons for interpretability while preserving latent richness. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2510.25807
- **[Hos25] Transcoder-based Circuit Analysis for Interpretable Single-Cell Foundation Models** — Hosokawa et al. (Univ. Tokyo); arXiv, 2025-09-18. Model: **Cell2Sentence (C2S)**. Method: transcoder → extract decision circuits. Finding: discovered circuits correspond to real biological mechanisms/pathways — first transcoder application to a scFM. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2509.14723
- **[Sch24] Can sparse autoencoders make sense of gene expression latent variable models?** — Schuster (MLCB), 2024-10. Earliest SAE-on-cell-models work; on simulated + single-cell multi-omics, SAEs recover generative variables in superposition (but unidentifiable if generative variables unknown); automated feature→concept linking. Link: arxiv.org/abs/2410.11468
- **[Orl26] What Do Biological Foundation Models Compute? SAEs from Feature Recovery to Mechanistic Interpretability** — Orlov et al.; bioRxiv, 2026-03-06. **Systematic review** of sparse dictionary learning across protein/DNA/single-cell/pathology FMs. Key argument: convergent feature recovery across architectures, **but validation is almost entirely annotation-matching → circularity risk**; proposes a 3-level framework (representational / computational / causal-mechanistic) and argues the field's opportunity is experimental validation (DMS, MPRA, structure). DOI: 10.64898/2026.03.04.709491
- **[Sha26] Ground-truth evaluation of SAE features on spatial, single-cell and language-model embeddings** — Shahsavand et al.; bioRxiv, 2026-10-29. Uses embeddings with *known* concepts (Geneformer single-cell with cell-lineage concepts; spatial CRC; synthetic planted states). Finding: **reproducibility ≠ meaningfulness**; single SAE fits cover most lineages but reproduce only 27–46% of features; a 5-fit consensus is both reproducible (78–98%) and pure. Direct methodological critique of seed-reproducibility as a trust test. DOI: 10.64898/2026.10.27.762296
- **[Gua25] SAEs Reveal Interpretable Structure in Small Gene Language Models** — Guan, He, Zhang; 2025-07. Shows even small gene LMs encode SAE-recoverable TF-binding motifs. Link: arxiv.org/abs/2507.07486

## 3. Single-cell FMs — attention analysis, probing & concept attribution

- **[Zha26] How different AI models understand cells differently (scGeneLens)** — Zhao, Hao, …, **X.-G. Zhang** (Tsinghua); bioRxiv, 2026-01-30. Models: scFoundation, scGPT. Method: sparse block attention + attention propagation across layers + integrated gradients disentangling gene-identity vs expression contributions. Finding: **pluralistic perception** — scFoundation emphasises marker-gene relations (stronger cell-type separability); scGPT emphasises shared-pathway genes (better cross-condition generalisation). DOI: 10.64898/2026.01.29.702682
- **[Jaf26] Attribution-based diagnostics for single-cell foundation models** — Jafari, Lazard, Conard, Coelho de Castro et al. (**Microsoft Research**); arXiv, 2026-11-02 (very recent). Models: **Cell2Sentence, scGPT, and scGPT+STATE** (perturbation). Method: post-hoc attribution for cell-type annotation + perturbation prediction vs regulatory ground truth (synthetic). Finding: attribution reveals marker-gene reliance, contextual interactions and shortcuts not captured by accuracy; **crucially, attribution conclusions depend strongly on choice of biological reference, baseline and null model**. Link: arxiv.org/abs/2611.02335
- **[Ped00] Identifying Biological Priors and Structure in Single-Cell Foundation Models** — Pedrocchi, Stark, Rätsch, Joudaki; (year unknown, workshop). Models: **Geneformer and UCE**. Methods: attention rollout, linear probes (where concepts appear), hidden-state comparison showing learning progression / token-pattern emergence. Link: semanticscholar.org/paper/103d44e8a2df32f67099c0b8ab523edc9898dbda
- **[Spe25b] Transformers Enhance the Predictive Power of Network Medicine** — Spector … **Barabási**; medRxiv, 2025-01-28. Model: Geneformer. Finding: gene-embedding cosine similarity and attention weights are elevated for experimentally validated **protein-protein interactions**; weighting PPI networks with them improves disease-gene detection and drug repurposing (dilated cardiomyopathy). Positive "attention captures real biology" result (for PPIs, not causal GRNs). DOI: 10.1101/2025.01.27.25321204
- **[Ota26b] Central Dogma Transformer II: an AI microscope for understanding cellular regulatory mechanisms** — Ota; Bioinformatics Advances, 2026-02-09. Architecture mirrors central dogma (DNA/RNA self-attention + DNA→RNA cross-attention). On K562 CRISPRi (5 genes held out): predicts perturbation effects (per-gene r=0.84), recovers the GFI1B network (6.6× enrichment), concentrates cross-attention on ENCODE CTCF sites. Positive interpretable-by-construction result. DOI: 10.1093/bioadv/vbag268 (predecessor **[Ota26]** Central Dogma Transformer, arXiv 2604.… )

## 4. Representation & manifold geometry — identity, pseudotime, lineage, TF→target directions

- **[Ken26f] Discovery of a Hematopoietic Manifold in scGPT …** — Kendiukhov; arXiv, 2026-03-10. Finding: scGPT internally encodes a **compact hematopoietic manifold with developmental branch structure**; a 3-stage extraction (frozen attention operator export + adaptor + readout) yields a standalone algorithm beating scVI/Palantir/DPT/CellTypist on **pseudotime-depth ordering** and subtype AUROC (CD4/CD8 0.867; mono/macro 0.951). A concrete "lineage hierarchy as geometry" + usable-algorithm-extraction result. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2603.10261
- **[Ken26m] Multi-Dimensional Spectral Geometry of Biological Knowledge in Single-Cell Transformer Representations** — Kendiukhov; arXiv, 2026-02-24. scGPT. Finding: genes organised into a structured coordinate system — dominant spectral axis = subcellular localisation (secreted↔cytosolic); intermediate layers transiently encode mitochondrial/ER compartments mirroring the secretory pathway; a 6-D subspace separates TFs from targets (AUROC 0.744); **repression edges geometrically more prominent than activation**; B-cell master regulators (BATF, BACH2) converge toward the PAX5 identity anchor across depth. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2602.22247
- **[Ken26k] What topological and geometric structure do biological foundation models learn? Evidence from 141 hypotheses** — Kendiukhov; PLOS One, 2026-02-25. scGPT, Geneformer; AI-driven hypothesis screening w/ null controls. Finding: genuine non-trivial topology (persistent homology significant in 11–12/12 layers); scGPT↔Geneformer agree on "shape" of gene space (CCA 0.80) but not gene placement; robust signal concentrates in immune tissue, fragile in lung under stringent nulls. DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0344826
- **[Ken26j] Residual-stream geometry … carries incremental gene-regulatory signal across tissues** — Kendiukhov; BMC Bioinformatics, 2026-07-24. Finding: geometric proximity adds incremental TRRUST-edge signal beyond expression (kidney ΔAUROC +0.122, immune +0.042) **but collapses to ~0 under leave-both-out (neither TF nor target shared)** — marks the ceiling of out-of-entity generalisation; apparent Geneformer>scGPT advantage mostly an extraction artifact. DOI: 10.1186/s12859-026-06538-5
- **[Gil25b] The cell as a token: high-dimensional geometry in language models and cell embeddings** — Gilpin; Bioinformatics, 2025. Connects LLM-token geometry to cell embeddings; representation-geometry framing. DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/btaf595
- **[Civ26b] Intermediate Layers Encode Optimal Biological Representations in scFMs** — Civale, …, Magi; arXiv, 2026-04-16. Models: scFoundation (100M), Tahoe-X1 (1.3B). Finding: optimal layer is task- and context-dependent — **trajectory inference peaks at ~60% depth (31% above final layer)**; perturbation optima shift 0–96% across T-cell activation states; first-layer best in quiescent cells. "Where you read matters as much as what is learned." DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2604.14838
- **[Wen26b] Beyond the Final Layer: Evaluating scFM Representations for Molecular Prediction** — Wen, Lyu, Robbins; bioRxiv, 2026-10-12. Geneformer, scBERT, scGPT, scFoundation; layer-wise probes, LODO, CKA, collapse analysis. Finding: intermediate layers improve cell-type accuracy for scGPT/scBERT; layer-1 earliest-on-par for perturbation; readout (CLS vs mean-pool) changes preferred depth. Corroborates Civ26b. DOI: 10.64898/2026.10.05.756880
- **[Wha26] Do Geometric Outliers Identify Important Genes in Single-Cell Foundation Models?** — Whalley; bioRxiv, 2026-06-22. Tests whether embedding-geometry outliers flag functionally important genes. DOI: 10.64898/2026.06.22.733850 (abstract not fully retrieved — **UNVERIFIED** beyond title)

## 5. Causal regulatory logic vs co-expression — CRISPR-grounded audits & in-silico-perturbation confounds

This is the field's sharpest front, and the results are predominantly **cautionary/negative**.

- **[Ken26g] Systematic Evaluation … Attention Captures Co-Expression Rather Than Unique Regulatory Signal** — Kendiukhov; arXiv, 2026-02-19 (most-cited of the cluster, 12 cites). Framework: 37 analyses, 153 tests; scGPT + Geneformer; K562/RPE1; CRISPRi/CRISPRa. Finding: attention has layer-specific structure (PPIs early, transcriptional regulation late) **but adds no incremental value for perturbation prediction** — trivial gene-level baselines (variance, mean, dropout) win (AUROC 0.81–0.88 vs 0.70); pairwise edges add ~0; ablating "regulatory heads" causes no degradation. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2602.17532
- **[Ken26h]** journal version in **BMC Genomics**, 2026-07-22 (same result, formalised Objective-A GRN-recovery vs Objective-B perturbation-target prediction; CSSI improves curated-GRN recovery up to 1.85×). DOI: 10.1186/s12864-026-12965-8
- **[Ken26i] Causal intervention validation of gene regulatory signals in scGPT** — J. Biomedical Informatics, 2026-07-03. Ablate/swap TF tokens inside scGPT. Finding (two axes): Axis 1 — intervention scores align with TRRUST/DoRothEA robustly in **lung** (not kidney at scale, not immune); scGPT's lead over 12 classical GRN methods is a **signal-efficiency** effect that narrows with more cells. Axis 2 — against real CRISPR (Adamson/Dixit/Shifrut), **AUROC ≈ 0.50: model-internal signal does not predict real perturbation outcomes**. "Representational, not biologically causal." DOI: 10.1016/j.jbi.2026.105080
- **[Ken26d] Causal circuit tracing … inhibitory dominance, biological coherence, cross-model convergence** — Bioinformatics, 2026-03-02. Model-internal SAE-feature ablation circuits (96,892 edges). Finding: edges share ontology terms 2.9–6.2× over null; inhibitory dominance 65–89%; 1,142 architecture-invariant GO-pairs shared scGPT↔Geneformer; circuit edges explain <1% of driver-gene co-expression variance (structure beyond bivariate correlation); but gene-level CRISPRi validation excess over baseline is tiny (+0.03 to +0.35 pp). DOI: 10.1093/bioinformatics/btag379
- **[Ken26e] Exhaustive Circuit Mapping … Layer-Dependent Differentiation Control** — arXiv, 2026-03-12. Geneformer. Three experiments: exhaustive tracing of all 4,065 layer-5 SAE features (1.39M edges, 27× more than selective sampling) reveals **heavy-tailed hubs, 40% of top hubs unannotated → annotation bias in prior work**; 3-way ablation shows deepening redundancy, zero synergy (subadditive); **trajectory-guided feature steering establishes causal layer→differentiation-direction link** (late-layer L17 features push toward maturity, fraction-positive=1.0; early/mid push away). One of the most direct "differentiation as steerable direction" results. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2603.11940
- **[Qiu26] A confound-diagnostic toolkit for in silico perturbation with scFMs** — Qiu & Zhao; bioRxiv, 2026-08-07. Geneformer-style gene-token deletion ISP. Finding: native embedding-delta gives **no reproducible held-out improvement beyond gene identity**; apparent effects trace to library-size structure, universal responsiveness, tokenization coverage, and circular state-scoring. Reusable matched-control framework for deciding when ISP readouts warrant biological interpretation. DOI: 10.64898/2026.08.04.732812
- **[Ye26] Benchmarking Static GRN Reconstruction and Dynamic Transition Probing in scFMs** — Ye, …, Chao Tang; bioRxiv, 2026-05-20 (top-ranked in target search). Disentangles 3 signal sources (token embeddings / final hidden states / attention) across 6 scFMs. Finding: **zero-shot scGPT token-embedding similarity beats classical baselines** on STRING/ChIP-seq and recovers core TFs; introduces **dynamic transition probing** (iterate reconstruction head to drive early→late states) where **scFoundation captures developmental transitions best**. More optimistic than the Kendiukhov audits — recovery depends on architecture, pretraining, extraction strategy. DOI: 10.64898/2026.05.17.725083
- **[Qi26] Towards Universal GRN Inference … Generalizable Regulatory Knowledge in scFMs** — Qi et al.; arXiv, 2026-05. Argues scFMs hold recoverable generalizable regulatory knowledge; method to unlock it. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2605.08128
- **[Ken26o] Partial-label metric ceilings for evaluating GRNs inferred from scFMs** — Bio Systems, 2026. Shows curated-reference GRN metrics have ceilings imposed by partial labelling — methodological caution for all the above. DOI: 10.1016/j.biosystems.2026.105864

## 6. Cell fate / differentiation / lineage dynamics expressed in model internals

- **[Zho26b] Benchmarking zero-shot scFM embeddings for cellular dynamics reconstruction** — X.-Y. Zhou, …, **Peijie Zhou**; bioRxiv, 2026-03-12. scFMs + dynamic optimal transport on branching lineages. Finding: **zero-shot scFM embeddings underperform a highly-variable-gene baseline** at backtracking progenitors, interpolating intermediates, extrapolating fates; mechanistic analysis finds scFMs **over-compress temporal signal → artificial "linearization" of branched structure** that obscures divergence points. Key "temporal-compression bottleneck" result for fate. DOI: 10.64898/2026.03.10.710748
- **[Cha25b] Reinforcement learning enables scFMs to learn cellular differentiation (DiRL)** — Chang, Chen, Liu; bioRxiv, 2025-12-12. Frames differentiation as goal-conditioned sequential decision-making over scFM latent space. Finding: outperforms random-perturbation baseline (69% win rate); learned policies recover known sequential Wnt/Hedgehog regulators; **critic value function recapitulates pseudotime ordering**. Interpretable fate-steering via RL on FM internals. DOI: 10.64898/2025.12.09.693267
- **[The23] Transfer learning enables predictions in network biology (Geneformer)** — **Theodoris** et al.; Nature, 2023-06. Foundational: introduced Geneformer and **in-silico deletion/activation perturbation** to rank network-central/cell-state-determining genes, validated experimentally (e.g., cardiomyopathy). The origin of the "in-silico perturbation for fate/therapeutic targets" paradigm that later work audits. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06139-9
- **[Zha26y] Discovery of candidate therapeutic targets with Geneformer** — Y.-J. Zhang, Venkatesh, **Theodoris**; Nature Protocols, 2026-04-23. Protocol for in-silico-perturbation-based target discovery. (Abstract unavailable in index — details **UNVERIFIED** beyond title/venue/lab.) DOI: 10.1038/s41596-026-01364-8
- **[Ort26] Temporal AI model predicts drivers of cell state trajectories across human aging** — Gómez Ortega, …, **Theodoris**; bioRxiv, 2026-03-30. Temporal extension predicting drivers of aging cell-state trajectories. DOI: 10.64898/2026.03.30.715396
- **[Wan24h] RegVelo: gene-regulatory-informed dynamics of single cells** — W. Wang, Weiler, Lange, …, **F. Theis**; bioRxiv, 2024-12-11. Not a FM-internals paper, but the strongest *interpretable dynamical* alternative: joint model of splicing kinetics + GRN enabling in-silico perturbation. Finding: in zebrafish neural crest, **RegVelo's in-silico predictions were validated by CRISPR/Cas9 knockout and Perturb-seq**, establishing tfec/elf1 in pigment-cell fate. Benchmark for what experimentally-grounded causal fate control looks like. DOI: 10.1101/2024.12.11.627935
- **[Sad24] Fatecode: cell fate regulator prediction using classification-supervised autoencoder perturbation** — Sadria, …, **Bader**; Cell Reports Methods, 2024. In-silico perturbation of a supervised autoencoder's latent space to nominate fate regulators. DOI: 10.1016/j.crmeth.2024.100819
- **[Pan26b] From cell types to cell states: adapting foundation models for cellular plasticity** — Pan, …, Secrier; bioRxiv, 2026. Adapts FMs to represent plastic cell *states* rather than discrete types. DOI: 10.1101/2024.08.16.608311 (abstract not retrieved — **UNVERIFIED** beyond title)

## 7. In-silico perturbation & virtual-cell models — critical / negative benchmarks

These test whether scFM/virtual-cell models actually predict perturbation responses (a prerequisite for claiming causal regulatory understanding).

- **[Ahl25] Deep-learning-based gene perturbation effect prediction does not yet outperform simple linear baselines** — **Ahlmann-Eltze, Huber, Anders** (EMBL); Nature Methods, 2025-08 (288 cites — the field's reference negative result). 5 FMs + 2 DL models vs deliberately simple baselines for single/double perturbations: **none beat the baselines**; combinatorial predictions no better than additive; unseen-gene predictions no better than predicting the training mean. DOI: 10.1038/s41592-025-02772-6 (preprint **[Ahl25b]** bioRxiv 2025-02, DOI 10.1101/2025.02.… adds: a linear model pretrained on another *perturbation* dataset beats all — observational pretraining is the problem).
- **[Cse25] Benchmarking foundation cell models for post-perturbation RNA-seq prediction** — Csendes, …, Szalai; BMC Genomics, 2025-04 (83 cites). scGPT & scFoundation vs baselines: **even "take the training mean" beats them**; biologically-featured basic ML beats scGPT by a large margin; current Perturb-seq benchmarks have low perturbation-specific variance. DOI: 10.1186/s12864-025-11600-2
- **[Wen25] PertEval-scFM** — Wenteler et al.; bioRxiv, 2025-05 (55 cites). Zero-shot scFM embeddings give **no consistent improvement** over baselines for perturbation effect, especially under distribution shift. DOI: 10.1101/2024.10.02.616248
- **[Li26i] A systematic comparison of single-cell perturbation response prediction models** — L.-X. Li, …, L.-Y. Tian; Science Advances, 2026-08. 13 methods × 25 datasets × 24 metrics. Finding: fine-tuned FMs **compress variance and underestimate synergy**; no method generalises across cell types; different metrics probe different biological signals. DOI: 10.1101/2024.12.23.630036
- **[Hei26b] Evaluating Single-Cell Perturbation Response Models Is Far from Straightforward** — Heidari et al.; bioRxiv, 2026-02. Shows widely-used metrics (Wasserstein, energy distance, correlations) are confounded by scale/sparsity/dimensionality and often misrepresent performance; complex DL often underperforms simple baselines. Metric-level critique. DOI: 10.64898/2026.02.14.705879
- **[Tiw26] Genetic Perturbation Prediction Models Fail Out-of-Distribution (EL-PERTURB)** — Tiwari et al.; bioRxiv, 2026-10. 20 models, stratified OOD splits: performance degrades substantially OOD; **scFM embeddings confer no reliable advantage** over tuned simple baselines. DOI: 10.64898/2026.10.16.759604
- **[Liu25b] Evaluating Foundation Models for In-Silico Perturbation (scISP)** — Liu et al.; bioRxiv, 2025-05. Geneformer, scGPT, GEARS on cell-state separation / ISP accuracy / perturbed-gene MRR; documents model-specific strengths/limits. DOI: 10.1101/2025.05.11.653338
- **[Has25] Multimodal Benchmarking of Foundation Model Representations for Cellular Perturbation Response** — Hasanaj, …, E. Xing; bioRxiv, 2025-06. DOI: 10.1101/2025.06.26.661186
- **[Zha26b] A low-rank decoder bottleneck bounds reliability in foundation-model perturbation prediction** — Z.-Y. Zhang et al.; 2026-11 (very recent). Argues a structural (low-rank decoder) bottleneck caps achievable reliability. Link: arxiv.org/abs/2611.05849

## 8. Genomic sequence models — SAEs & feature discovery (Evo 2, Nucleotide Transformer, DNABERT, RNA LMs)

Included where interpretability concerns regulatory-element or cell-type-specific representation.

- **[Mai26] Decode-gLM: Tools to Interpret, Audit, and Steer Genomic Language Models** — Maiwald, …, **Schölkopf, Morris, Crook** (Oxford/MPI); bioRxiv, 2026-02 (posted 2025-10-31). Model: Nucleotide Transformer. SAEs recover >60 functional annotations unsupervised across layers/sizes; recovered a **CMV enhancer despite viral genomes being excluded → audited training-data contamination/leakage**; Meta-SAEs find conceptual hierarchies (HIV); random-init probing confirms features learned in pretraining; **steered the model toward the A1408G aminoglycoside-resistance mutation via an antibiotic-resistance feature**. Flagship "discovery + audit + steer" result. DOI: 10.1101/2025.10.31.685860
- **[Bri26] Genome modelling and design across all domains of life with Evo 2** — Brixi, …, **B. Hie** (Arc Institute); Nature, 2026-03-04 (and preprint **[Bri25]** bioRxiv 2025-02, 321 cites). Mechanistic-interpretability analyses report Evo 2 features for **exon–intron boundaries, TF binding sites, protein structural elements, prophage regions**; inference-time-steered chromatin-accessibility generation. The most visible genomic-FM interpretability claim; later papers stress-test it (below). DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10176-5 / 10.1101/2025.02.18.638918
- **[Ali26] Causal dictionary learning … transcription-factor binding features in genomic language models** — S. Ali; arXiv, 2026-07. Models: Nucleotide Transformer (6-mer) + DNABERT-2 (BPE). TopK SAEs → thousands of monosemantic TF-motif features. Finding: **naive PWM validation is severely confounded by GC content and repeats (hundreds of spurious "TF features")**; a composition-matched, binding-resolved protocol + **decoder-ablation during the forward pass** establishes features *causally* used to represent cell-type-specific TF binding (CTCF/GATA1/REST; 7–14 of 15 features validate; two negative-control classes give no signal). Sets a reusable causal standard. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2607.19618
- **[Kmi26] Hidden State Genomics: Graph-Based Analysis of SAE Feature Activity in Genomic LMs** — Kmiec, O'Brien, McCoy; bioRxiv, 2026-05. Nucleotide Transformer v2 (500M), SAEs on all 24 layers. Finding: correlation-based annotation inconsistent across layers; typed sequence→feature knowledge graphs + decoder interventions show **asymmetric effects** (suppressive features collapse signal; binding features shift predictions cumulatively). gLM reps align more with **local biophysical/syntactic constraints than with distributed regulatory logic**. DOI: 10.64898/2026.05.13.725007
- **[Mcl26] Probing genomic LMs: Nucleotide-GPT and the role of pretraining** — McLaughlin & Lim (UCSF); Briefings in Bioinformatics, 2026-01. Decoder-only single-nucleotide gLM; linear probing + SAEs while varying repetitive-element weighting. Finding: moderate RE downweighting optimal; **SAE features are dominated by repetitive-element patterns → REs may dominate the pretraining objective**, a key challenge for learning functional (vs ubiquitous-repeat) syntax. DOI: 10.1093/bib/bbag011

## 13. Genuine gaps (areas that look under-addressed)

1. **SAEs/transcoders tied to *fate decisions* specifically.** SAE work recovers cell-type and pathway programs, but almost nothing decomposes the *bifurcation / fate-commitment* computation itself into features and tests them against lineage-tracing ground truth (`Ken26e` steering is the closest; no cross-group replication). Lineage tracing (LARRY/Weinreb-style) is essentially unused as an interpretability ground truth.
2. **Causal validation against CRISPR for the newest/largest models.** The CRISPR-grounded audits target scGPT/Geneformer. **Virtual-cell / perturbation-native models (STATE, scGPT+STATE, TranscriptFormer, CellFM, large Tahoe-X1) are essentially un-audited** for causal regulatory logic — `Jaf26` touches scGPT+STATE but only in a synthetic setting.
3. **Cross-group replication of the central negative results.** The "attention/ISP = co-expression, not causal" conclusion rests heavily on one author (Kendiukhov cluster) plus `Qiu26`. Independent replication at scale is missing.
4. **Cell2Sentence / LLM-based cell models.** Only `Hos25` (transcoder) and `Jaf26` (attribution) interpret C2S; no SAE feature atlas, no steering/circuit tracing of the generative text-to-cell pathway, despite C2S being a natural target for LLM-interpretability transfer.
5. **UCE, CellFM, Nicheformer internals.** UCE's emergent developmental-lineage behaviour (`Ros26c`) is described at the output level but its *internal* representation of lineage/fate is unprobed; CellFM has essentially no interpretability study; Nicheformer's spatial/niche representation is evaluated by linear probing only (`Tej24`), not mechanistically.
6. **Linking single-cell FM internals to sequence/regulatory-model internals.** `Con25` and `Par26e` gesture at a bridge, but there is no unified account of whether a scFM's "regulatory" features correspond to the cis-regulatory grammar that Enformer/Borzoi/AlphaGenome encode.
7. **Steering with experimental validation.** Feature-steering results (`Ped26` drug features, `Mai26` resistance mutation, `Ken26e` maturity) are validated against model-internal or annotation targets, rarely against *new wet-lab* outcomes (contrast `Wan24h`/`Par26e`, which do validate). Prospective experimental validation of a steered cell-fate prediction is an open frontier.
8. **AlphaGenome / Borzoi SAEs.** SAE dictionary learning has hit Evo 2 and Nucleotide Transformer but, as far as this survey found, **not Enformer/Borzoi/AlphaGenome embeddings** — a clear methodological gap given these are the cell-type-specific regulatory workhorses.
